About Treaulab

An independent studio building quiet, long-lived software.

Who we are

Treaulab is a small independent software studio. We design, build, and operate our own web services and infrastructure tooling rather than reselling or brokering third-party platforms. The work here is deliberately narrow: fewer projects, maintained for years, documented in public where possible.

How we work

  • Plain first: static HTML, system fonts, no frameworks or trackers unless a task genuinely requires them.
  • Reproducible operations: servers and deployments are defined in version control, so any machine can be rebuilt from scratch.
  • Self-hosting by default: our sites run on infrastructure we control, served over HTTPS with strict security headers.
  • Honest scope: we do not do advertising technology, data brokerage, bulk mail, or growth hacking.

Why this domain exists

treau.me is our canonical home on the web. It hosts studio information only — documentation, contact details, and policy pages. Operational or experimental services run on separate subdomains so this apex domain stays a stable, easily categorized business presence.
