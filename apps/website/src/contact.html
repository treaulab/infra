Contact

Real addresses, monitored by a human. No forms, no trackers.

Addresses

  • General inquiries: hello@treau.me
  • Abuse reports (spam, phishing, copyright): abuse@treau.me — include URLs, headers, and timestamps
  • Security disclosures: see security.txt; please use a descriptive subject line and avoid sending exploits

Response times

We aim to acknowledge general mail within a few business days and abuse or security reports within 48 hours. If a matter is urgent, prefix the subject with [abuse] or [security].

Before you write

This site sends no bulk email and hosts no accounts, so there is no password reset or billing support queue. For open-source questions, include the repository name and version you are running.
