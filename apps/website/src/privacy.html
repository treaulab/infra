Privacy

No trackers, no cookies, no analytics. Last updated 2026-10-14.

What we do not collect

This site loads no third-party scripts, sets no cookies, and runs no analytics or fingerprinting. Reading these static pages requires no account and provides us no profile about you.

What we do process

We do not sell, share, or broker personal data, and we send no bulk email from this domain.
