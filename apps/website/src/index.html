What we do

  • Small web services — design, build, and operate focused services that do one thing well, with plain HTML first and minimal dependencies.

How we operate

We run our own infrastructure on version-controlled configuration, serve static content over HTTPS with strict transport security, and avoid third-party trackers entirely. This site sets no cookies, loads no external scripts, and collects no analytics — see our privacy note.
